ABOUT AMI

Good people.
Good actions. Good change.

Our Land. Our People. Our Responsibility.

Our vision

To help build a compassionate, environmentally conscious and culturally vibrant society through collective action and community participation.

Our mission

AMI – The Good Circle aims to bring people together around initiatives that care for nature, support communities and celebrate culture. We believe positive change grows when people contribute their time, creativity, knowledge and care.

Our values

Responsibility

Care for our land, communities and shared future.

Compassion

Listen, include and support people with dignity.

Creativity

Use art, ideas and culture to inspire positive action.
