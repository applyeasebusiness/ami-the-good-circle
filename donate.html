Before launching online donations

  • Use a payment gateway account registered to the organization, not a personal account.
  • Publish a donation policy, refund/cancellation policy, privacy notice and official contact details.
  • Issue acknowledgements/receipts accurately and only make tax-benefit claims where legally applicable.
  • For foreign contributions, obtain qualified guidance on applicable FCRA requirements before accepting them.
