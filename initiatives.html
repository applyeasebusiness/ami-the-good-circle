WHAT WE CARE ABOUT

Three themes.
One shared purpose.

We bring people together through practical, locally relevant activities.

01 / NATURE

Protect our natural heritage

Potential activities include wildlife awareness, habitat conservation education, waste reduction and community environmental initiatives. Publish specific projects and outcomes only after they have taken place and are verified.

02 / SOCIETY

Build stronger communities

Potential activities include community support, volunteering, awareness programs and helping communities prepare for and recover from difficult events.

03 / CULTURE

Celebrate art and Assamese identity

Creative activities can help young people express ideas, learn about heritage and participate in conversations about the future of Assam. Art for Assam 2026 is a featured initiative.
